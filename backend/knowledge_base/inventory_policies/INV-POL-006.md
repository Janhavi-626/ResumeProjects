# Returned Inventory Disposition
Document ID: INV-POL-006 | Version: 1.4 | Effective date: 2025-01-10

## Section 6.1: Inspection required
Damaged, opened, or incomplete goods must be routed to inspection or quarantine. Available stock does not prove a replacement is reserved.

## Section 6.2: Replacement availability
Inventory is informational until a reservation tool confirms allocation. Do not promise replacement shipment based only on on-hand quantity.

## Section 6.3: Unsafe goods
Products with safety concerns must not be returned to sellable inventory. Escalate to product safety operations.

## Section 6.4: Inventory evidence
Report product identifier, warehouse, on-hand count, and disposition from the inventory tool. Do not infer a disposition from product category.
