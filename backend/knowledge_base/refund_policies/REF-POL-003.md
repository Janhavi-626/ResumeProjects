# Refund Processing
Document ID: REF-POL-003 | Version: 4.0 | Effective date: 2025-03-01

## Section 4.1: Refund timing
After a refund is submitted, settlement commonly takes 5 to 10 business days depending on the payment provider. A pending payment record is not proof that funds have settled.

## Section 4.2: Refund requests
Only an authorized payment workflow may submit a refund. Support agents may create a refund review request; they must not represent a refund as issued until a payment tool returns a confirmed result.

## Section 4.3: Partial refunds
Refund amount must not exceed the verified captured amount less previously refunded amounts. A reviewer must approve any exception or compensation.

## Section 4.4: Duplicate refund protection
Check payment status and idempotency history before submitting a refund request. Never submit the same refund request twice.
