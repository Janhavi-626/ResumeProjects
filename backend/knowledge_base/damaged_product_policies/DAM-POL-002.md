# Damaged Product Handling
Document ID: DAM-POL-002 | Version: 2.1 | Effective date: 2025-02-15

## Section 2.1: Damage reported in the return window
Record the customer's description and available shipment evidence. Set returned inventory to inspection or quarantine; never classify a reported damaged item as sellable without inspection.

## Section 2.2: Damage reported after the return window
Delivery damage reported after the standard window is an exception requiring human review. Compare the claim with delivery scans and prior case notes. Do not reject solely because the standard window elapsed.

## Section 2.3: Evidence handling
Use tool-verified order, shipment, and return data as operational facts. Customer statements are claims until independently verified. Store only concise evidence needed for case handling.

## Section 2.4: Resolution authority
Refunds, replacements, and compensation are proposals until an authorized human approves them and the corresponding tool confirms the request was recorded.
