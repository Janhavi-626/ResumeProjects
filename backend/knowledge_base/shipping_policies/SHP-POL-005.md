# Shipment Investigation
Document ID: SHP-POL-005 | Version: 2.3 | Effective date: 2025-02-01

## Section 5.1: Lost shipment
A carrier status of lost or no delivery scan requires a shipment investigation. A carrier status is evidence, not confirmation of refund eligibility.

## Section 5.2: Delivery date
Use the verified delivery scan when calculating return windows. Do not infer delivery from order date or estimated delivery date.

## Section 5.3: Wrong destination
Escalate conflicting delivery address or proof-of-delivery details to the shipping operations team. Do not reveal full delivery address in customer-facing output.

## Section 5.4: Delayed carrier events
Carrier delays may support an exception review. Record tracking event timestamps and keep the customer updated without promising a financial outcome.
