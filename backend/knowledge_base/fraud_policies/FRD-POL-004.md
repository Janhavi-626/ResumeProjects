# Returns Risk Review
Document ID: FRD-POL-004 | Version: 1.7 | Effective date: 2025-01-20

## Section 1.1: Risk indicators
Repeated high-value returns, mismatches between customer and order data, or conflicting shipment evidence may require specialist review. A risk indicator is not a finding of fraud.

## Section 1.2: Restricted decisions
Do not deny service, alter an account, or disclose risk signals solely from an automated score. Escalate relevant evidence to an authorized reviewer.

## Section 1.3: Minimum data
Include only the minimum order and return evidence needed for review. Do not expose internal risk labels in customer-facing language.

## Section 1.4: Human review
All cases with a recorded risk flag must be escalated. Automated systems may organize facts but cannot make a fraud determination.
